### natelalor.com
As of August 2026, this website has a new playful look. It is a home base for a couple things: 

First, place to learn about me and a hub for all of my social/professional profiles. You can follow me on Instagram, Medium, GitHub, and connect with me on LinkedIn. I hope to add more links in the future, maybe featuring additional profiles like Strava, Duolingo, or other Instagram accounts I may have in the works (A more engaging Instagram API integration, perhaps???).

Second, it is a place to showcase my professional skill set and my past/current projects. I take pride in what I am passionate about and the skill development I achieve in my free time. From online certs to side-projects, I love learning and I want this website to be a place to show what I'm currently learning more about.

Third, it is a place where I can be creative. I love to draw and I wanted this website to portray who I am: enthusiastic, inclusive, engaging, curious. Future developments of the website may have some secrets to uncover, or little side quests to complete. :)

Lastly, I also try to get out and do a ton of different things. Producing music, content creation, baking sourdough, experimenting with international cuisine, running, basketball, and everything in between. I want a section to be able to track my accolades or goals so I can keep track of what I've accomplished and what I am still working towards. This is still a work in progress, and will be merged once it is beautiful and ready-to-go.

I hope you enjoyed your journey through natelalor.com!
